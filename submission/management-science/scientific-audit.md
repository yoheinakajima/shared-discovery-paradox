Two-state diagnostic: N=M=2, uniform posterior. Identical independent strategies yield coverage 3/4 at the symmetric equilibrium; distinct assigned actions yield 1. Thus “optimal symmetric coverage” and “every pure equilibrium first-best” must not be collapsed into the same theorem. This example is elementary reasoning, not new research or an experimental result.
